Crisis & Mental Health

If you or a veteran you know is struggling, help is available right now. You are not alone and you are not a burden.

📞

Veterans Crisis Line — 988, Press 1

Free, confidential support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. You can also text 838255 or chat online. One call connects you to a trained VA responder who understands what you've been through.

📞 Call 988💬 Text 838255
SAFER Together — Firearm Safe Storage Program
About SAFER Together The Storing Ammunition and Firearms to Enhance Resilience Together (SAFER Together) program lets veterans, first responders, and civilians voluntarily and temporarily store firearms at participating gun shops during a personal crisis — no questions asked, completely confidential. When you feel ready, you pick them up during normal business hours. Peer support is available throughout the process. For resources and participating locations, visit vetsforhope.com

VA & Vet Centers

VA Medical Centers, Community Based Outpatient Clinics, and Vet Centers located across Alabama. Vet Centers are community-based counseling centers separate from the VA hospital system — no appointment needed for many services, and combat veterans do not need to enroll in VA healthcare to use them.

Alabama Veterans Resource Center (AVRC)
Brand New in 2026 Opened February 18, 2026 — the AVRC is a public-private partnership created by Gov. Kay Ivey and the Alabama Legislature to serve as a one-stop statewide hub for veterans navigating benefits, employment, housing, healthcare, and more. The Montgomery location is the first, with satellite offices planned statewide. An online digital hub is also live at missionforwardal.org.

VA Vet Centers in Alabama
What Is a Vet Center? VA Vet Centers are community-based readjustment counseling centers offering individual and group therapy, PTSD treatment, MST counseling, substance abuse referrals, employment assistance, and bereavement counseling — all in a non-clinical, community setting. Combat veterans can access Vet Center services regardless of VA enrollment status.

Housing Resources

Emergency shelter, transitional housing, permanent housing, and homebuying assistance for Alabama veterans at every stage of need.

HUD-VASH — Start Here If You Are Homeless The HUD-VA Supportive Housing program combines VA healthcare with HUD housing vouchers for veterans experiencing homelessness. Contact any Alabama VA Medical Center directly and ask specifically about HUD-VASH to get started. You do not need a VA disability rating to qualify.
📋

Benefits & Claims

Free claims assistance, disability ratings, appeals, PACT Act toxic exposure benefits, and state-specific Alabama veteran benefits.

Never Pay to File a VA Claim Accredited VSO representatives, ADVA county service officers, American Legion, DAV, and VFW service officers all help veterans file and appeal VA claims at no cost. If anyone asks for money upfront to file a claim, walk away — it may be a scam.
