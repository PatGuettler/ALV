Alabama Veteran Initiative

Built for Vets.
By Vets.

Too many veterans lose their sense of purpose and community after service ends. The transition to civilian life can be isolating — and that isolation has real consequences for mental health and well-being.

AV Active is a digital hub designed around the way veterans actually live. It brings together peer communication, fitness tracking, wellness resources, PTG programming, and real community events in one place — keeping Alabama vets engaged in a positive, productive way.

Corporate sponsors can connect with and support veterans through the app, helping track impact and fund activities that make a real difference.

What AV Active Focuses On

The Five Pillars

Every feature in AV Active is built around these five commitments to the veteran community.

💬
Community

Stay connected with fellow Alabama vets, share experiences, and build the kind of brotherhood and sisterhood that doesn't end at ETS.

💪
Health & Fitness

Track workouts, join challenges, and stay accountable with other vets who understand what it means to push past the hard days.

🧠
Well-Being

Access mental wellness resources, PTG programming, and tools that support the whole person — not just the physical side of recovery.

🌱
PTG & Growth

Post-Traumatic Growth is at the heart of everything Alabama Veteran does. AV Active brings that framework into your daily life.

📅
Events & Challenges

From group rucks to War on the Greens, AV Active keeps you plugged into events and monthly challenges built for Alabama veterans.
