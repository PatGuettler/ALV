Annual Golf Tournament · Fundraiser
War on the Greens
📅  October 19, 2026 · 7:30 AM – 3:30 PM
📍  Inverness Country Club · Birmingham, AL

Honoring Alabama's fallen heroes, one swing at a time. The War on the Greens pairs veterans with players to foster camaraderie, share stories, and showcase the value of those who served. Each year, the event is dedicated to a Gold Star family — ensuring their loved one's sacrifice is never forgotten. Great golf, a powerful mission, and a community that shows up.

Annual Gala · Fundraiser
Salute to Service
📅  2027 — Date Coming Soon
📍  Birmingham, AL
Something Bigger Is Coming in 2027 We are revamping the Salute to Service Gala for 2027 — a bigger night, a bolder mission, and an experience worthy of the veterans we serve. Stay connected and be the first to know when details drop.

The Salute to Service Gala is Alabama Veteran's premier fundraising evening — bringing together community leaders, supporters, and veterans for a night of recognition, impact, and celebration. Every dollar raised goes directly to funding retreats, resources, and programs for Alabama's veterans.
