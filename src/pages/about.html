Our Mission

HELPING VETERANS FIND PURPOSE AFTER SERVICE.

Alabama Veteran exists to empower and uplift our veteran community and their families across the state.

We stand ready to guide every veteran in Alabama with access and direction to a wide range of resources so they can connect with other Veterans, engage in meaningful volunteer activities, and successfully navigate their next mission: civilian life.

Since 2016, we have been 100% sustained by volunteers and donations. We're a Platinum Transparency charity — whatever you give, it goes right to the mission.

Leadership

Board of Directors

The people who show up every day to make sure Alabama's veterans are never left behind.

President
Chris Montz
Sr. Loan Officer · Edge Home Finance

A founding board member since 2017, Chris comes from a family of veterans and has spent over two decades specializing in VA home loans. He leads Alabama Veteran with a personal mission rooted in honor, service, and the belief that no veteran should navigate civilian life alone.

Vice President
Chris 'Elroy' Stricklin
Air Force Colonel (ret) · Business Executive & Speaker

A three-time bestselling author and nationally recognized keynote speaker, Elroy served 23 years in the Air Force — from F-15C and the Thunderbirds to DARPA and the Pentagon, with combat tours in Iraq and Afghanistan. He is a passionate advocate for veteran mental health.

Secretary
Jesse Elders
US Navy Reserves

An insurance and operations professional with a background in claims, Jesse is a Navy Seabee veteran who continues to serve in the Naval Reserves. He brings operational discipline and a ground-level veteran perspective to everything the organization does.

Parliamentarian
Dylan Angeline
Corporate Counsel · Community Health Systems

An Air Force veteran and attorney specializing in business litigation and healthcare advisory services, Dylan has served as a senior health policy analyst in Washington, D.C. He also serves on the board of Dovetail Landing, supporting behavioral health services for veterans and their families.

Board Member
James "Mike" Oakley
Mayor · Centreville, AL

Mike served nearly 40 years in the Alabama Army National Guard, retiring as Command Sergeant Major — the highest enlisted rank in the U.S. Armed Forces. Elected Mayor of Centreville in 2020, his career spans military service, journalism, education, and public service at every level of Alabama government.

Board Member
Danny Noles
Founder & CEO · The Noles Group

Danny grew up in Roanoke, Alabama and attended Auburn University through the ROTC program before serving in the United States Army. His military service shaped the discipline and leadership that define his 30-year career in financial services. He founded The Noles Group in 2016, focusing on retirement and financial planning across Alabama. A supporter of Alabama Veteran since day one in 2016, Danny has served on the board for three years and brings a deep commitment to veterans and community to the table.
